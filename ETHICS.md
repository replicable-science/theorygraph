# Ethics

**Material.** Public stage pitches at a startup competition, by founders who chose to present in public. No interviews, no private records, no interaction with participants.

**Third-party speech.** Transcripts are not redistributed. Short passages of real pitches are not shown on the project page. The page shows model-written premise statements and labels, and the quoted passages of fictional pitches only.

**People.** We do not code, infer, or release any characteristic of presenters. Models are told to disregard what they may know about a venture, and the coding input carries a case identifier and a transcript only. We cannot prove a model disregarded outside knowledge; the audit measures disagreement between models, which would also show if some models drew on memory of well-known companies.

**Harms we see.**

* *A model's reading of a founder's reasoning can be wrong and travel as fact.* Every output in this repository is shown beside the passage it rests on and the result of the source check, and the human-coded benchmark is designed to measure how often models misread.
* *Premise or status labels could be used to rank founders.* The repository makes no claim about founders' quality. It ships each venture's status on TechCrunch's public listing and no characteristic of a presenter. The measures are not validated for any decision about individuals.
* *Models can reproduce the structure of the frameworks they were trained on* (for example business-model templates). The planned audit tests this directly.

**Data protection.** No personal data beyond what is said on public stages. Company names identify public recordings. A person named in a pitch can ask for removal from the shipped files by writing to the authors.

**Funding and conflicts.** Withheld for blind review.

**Review.** The ethics-board determination is withheld for blind review.
