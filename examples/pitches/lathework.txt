Hello everyone. There is a shortage of electricians and plumbers and at the same time a lot of very good retired tradespeople who miss working. Our idea is simple. Lathework connects retired tradespeople with vocational schools that need part time instructors. Most schools right now cannot find enough teachers, so they cap their class sizes, and that is where we come in. A retired electrician can teach one evening class a week from our platform, and the school pays us twenty percent of the instructor fee. We believe retirees will want this because they get paid and because they get to pass on what they know. We will start in three states where schools already have waivers for non-certified instructors. We think schools will prefer us to hiring adjuncts, because we handle background checks, scheduling and payroll in one place. In our first pilot with one community college outside Columbus, we placed eleven instructors in one semester and the college added two new class sections. We plan to add a training module for first time teachers so that quality stays high. The market is huge, there are over one thousand community colleges and four million retired tradespeople in the country, and demand for skilled trades is growing every year. Our team includes a former college dean and a software engineer. We are looking for a two million dollar seed round to expand to ten states. We will be at table four after this.
